# About Me

I currently live in the Charlotte, North Carolina Metropolitan Area, but have been in Washington, Arizona, and California area for a while. The Bagpiping has taken me a bit further than those areas. Across North America and all the way to Scotland. Currently, I work in Data Engineering for Lowe's.

### My Background
I grew up in Vancouver, Washington. Started Bagpiping at 10 years of age with the Sir James MacDonald Pipe Band from Mark Tomasseti. Then bounced around to Arizona for about a decade from High School to my early career. And have since moved to North Carolina since October, 2016.
