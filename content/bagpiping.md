# About My Piping

If I'm doing the math right, I've been playing the Bagpipes since 2002. Around the age of 10. And in the first few years, I took Piping lessons from Mark Tomasseti in Vancouver, Washington. After moving to Arizona early on in High School, I received valuable tuition from a variety of folks. Some very directly, others by observation. It's amazing what you can learn when you watch what other folks are doing!

Currently, I compete at the Grade 1 level in solo. In the U.S., this is the second highest level of solo Piping. And in Bands, I have competed at every single Grade level. Bands are definitely where I find the greatest joy!
